About Bloomora

A second opinion, not a guess.

Bloomora exists because most pregnancy diet advice online is either too generic to be useful, or too clinical to be practical. We combine both — medical rigor and everyday, regional food.

Medical guidelines

Nutrient targets and condition-specific rules are grounded in WHO, ICMR, the National Institute of Nutrition (India), NHS UK and USDA FoodData Central references.

Expert rule engine

Conditions like anemia, gestational diabetes or thyroid imbalance trigger specific, explainable adjustments — the same logic a dietitian would reach for.

Regional & seasonal sense

Recommendations lean on food that's actually in season and available in your region, from Gujarati thalis to North Eastern fermented greens.

Your privacy, respected

Nothing you type is stored, logged in to, or linked to you. Bloomora keeps a single anonymous counter of plans generated — that's all.

Where the guidance comes from

  • World Health Organization (WHO)
  • UNICEF & FAO nutrition guidance
  • Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)
  • National Institute of Nutrition, India
  • Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, India
  • Indian Food Composition Tables
  • USDA FoodData Central
  • NIH & PubMed peer-reviewed research
  • NHS UK & CDC
  • Traditional Ayurvedic dietary references

Bloomora is an educational tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace your doctor or registered dietitian.
